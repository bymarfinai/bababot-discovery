# SOL Decision Log

> Scientific decision ledger for the SOL pair-native lineage. The purpose is to prevent a fresh research session from reopening settled questions or promoting evidence beyond its tested scope.

## Status semantics

- `OPEN` — unresolved scientific question.
- `PROVISIONAL` — promising but not sufficiently replicated/frozen.
- `LOCKED` — accepted conclusion under the exact frozen scope.
- `REJECTED` — tested candidate/family failed its preregistered gate.
- `INCONCLUSIVE` — the tested family did not establish the claim; exact route is closed unless its reopen condition is met.

A `LOCKED` scientific conclusion is not automatically a production/live rule.
