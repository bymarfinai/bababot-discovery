# SOL Negative Results Register

> First-class register of failed, inconclusive, or strongly constrained SOL research routes. The purpose is to stop future sessions from rediscovering attractive ideas that the lineage has already falsified or failed to support.

## Rule

Before proposing a new SOL experiment, search this file for overlapping:

- causal premise;
- feature family;
- intervention shape;
- time horizon;
- comparator;
- rescue logic.

`DO NOT RETEST` means the **same scientific premise/family under the same frozen parent** must not be rerun merely with neighboring thresholds, horizons, or renamed features.

A route may reopen only if its explicit reopen condition is satisfied and the new rationale is preregistered before results are inspected.
