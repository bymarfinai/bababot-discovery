# BNB Session-Native London→New York LONG M7 Entry Economics Anatomy — B27ES Preregistration

## Purpose
Evaluate the actual trading economics of **all six causal B27EO entry candidates** between K1 leave and H2. This milestone is exploratory economics anatomy only; it does not promote a final live setup.

## Frozen data scope
- Symbol: `BNBUSDT`
- Base bars: raw 5m
- Partition: **development only**, `2022-01-01 <= timestamp < 2025-01-01`
- Expected upstream population: **97 causal K1 leaves**, including 76 upstream H2 and 21 upstream non-H2 from B27EM.
- External, reference-validation, August, SHORT and live data are excluded from candidate economics selection in this milestone.

## Frozen entry candidates
Reuse B27EO definitions without changes:
1. `E0_NEXT_OPEN`
2. `E1_FIRST_BULL_CLOSE`
3. `E2_F95_RECLAIM`
4. `E3_F90_RECLAIM`
5. `E4_F85_RECLAIM`
6. `E5_MICRO_HL_BULL`

Every eligible occurrence is included. No clean-reclaim filter or other post-B27EO filter is allowed.

## Execution timing
- Entry is at the B27EO executable 5m bar **open**.
- Economics tracking begins on that same entry bar because the order is assumed filled at the bar open.
- Horizon ends at New York session close of the same session.
- If TP and SL are both touched within the same 5m candle, **SL wins** (pessimistic intrabar convention).
- If neither barrier is touched by session end, exit at the final available 5m close.

## Frozen cost model
Reuse the repository BBC execution assumptions:
- round-trip fee: **0.0010 = 0.10%** of notional
- slippage allowance: **0.0005 = 0.05%** of notional
- total round-trip trading cost deducted from every completed trade: **0.0015 = 0.15%**

PnL is reported primarily as net return on notional. A secondary illustration uses:
- margin = `$10`
- leverage = `50x`
- illustrative notional = `$500`

No funding, liquidation model, maintenance margin, or portfolio concurrency is modeled here. Therefore the `$500` PnL is only a linear notional illustration, not a live-futures claim.

## Structural excursion anatomy
For every eligible entry, report from entry through NY close:
- MFE in `R` and percent of entry price
- MAE in `R` and percent of entry price
- maximum extension above `H` in `R`
- whether price reaches each fixed target:
  - `H + 0.00R`
  - `H + 0.05R`
  - `H + 0.10R`
  - `H + 0.20R`
  - `H + 0.30R`
  - `H + 0.50R`
- median time from entry to each reached target

## Frozen TP / SL grid
For LONG trades:
- TP price = `H + EXT_R * R`
- `EXT_R ∈ {0.00, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.50}`
- SL price = `entry_price - STOP_R * R`
- `STOP_R ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30, 0.50}`

This creates **36 fixed TP/SL cells per entry candidate**. No grid value may be added after results are observed.

## Per-grid-cell economics
Report at minimum:
- trades
- TP exits / SL exits / session-close exits
- same-candle TP+SL collisions
- gross win rate and net win rate after total cost
- average and median net return
- total and average illustrative PnL on `$500` notional
- profit factor on net PnL
- chronological max drawdown on `$500` illustrative notional
- median initial gross reward:risk ratio
- share of trades with initial gross RR >= 1.0

## Descriptive ranking contract
This is discovery, not validation.
For each candidate separately:
1. identify the highest-average-net-return grid cell;
2. also report the highest-average-net-return cell among cells whose **median initial gross RR >= 1.0**;
3. report how many of its 36 cells have positive net expectancy, and how many positive-expectancy cells also have median gross RR >= 1.0.

Across candidates, compare robustness using the count/share of positive-expectancy cells and the best RR>=1 cell. A single isolated best cell is not sufficient for promotion.

## Integrity / anti-look-ahead
- Candidate signals and fills must be generated by the frozen B27EO causal logic.
- No future candle may affect entry eligibility or fill price.
- The terminal/outcome labels from B27EO are descriptive only and must not gate economics inclusion.
- All actual eligible entries are simulated regardless of eventual H2/non-H2 status.
- No reference-validation, external, August, H3, breakout-retest, SHORT, live, funding, liquidation, or parameter retuning in B27ES.

## Stop rule
Persist complete detail, target-hit, grid-summary and result files, then stop. Any economics validation is a later separately frozen milestone.
