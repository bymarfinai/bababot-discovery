Research/shadow only. No live promotion or profit guarantee.

## Per-era robustness gate

A candidate must pass **all three historical eras independently**:

- N >= 20 trades in the era.
- WR >= 52%.
- net PnL > $0.
- expectancy > $0/trade.
- PF >= 1.05.
- max DD <= $125.
- max loss streak <= 8.

Additionally, at least **two of the three eras** must have WR >= 55%.

## Combined all-era gate

After concatenating the three historical eras chronologically, a candidate must also satisfy:

- N >= 120.
- WR >= 55%.
- net PnL > $0.
- expectancy >= $1.00/trade.
- PF >= 1.30.
- max DD <= $150.
- max loss streak <= 8.

These thresholds are frozen before the H23 exact-anchor scan is executed.

## Selection rule

Within each exact anchor:

1. Keep only full-gate passers.
2. Rank by the weakest-era robustness first:
   - highest minimum era WR;
   - highest minimum era expectancy;
   - highest combined WR;
   - highest combined expectancy;
   - lowest combined max DD;
   - larger N only as a final tie-breaker.
3. Select at most one primary character per exact anchor.
4. If an anchor has no full-gate passer, mark it `NO_ROBUST_CHARACTER`; do not relax gates.

The four anchors are not forced to share the same character, lookback, or hold.

## Scientific interpretation

A full-gate passer is an **all-era robust discovery candidate**, not an independently validated strategy. The already inspected August 2026 H23 06:45 shadow trades cannot be reused as clean validation for this H23 lineage.

Any future validation of selected candidates must use data after the discovery/inspection boundary and must freeze the candidate before that future outcome is evaluated.
