## Objective
After A21 exhausts the remaining untouched H2-dominant clock grammar, test a new and explicitly separate Stage-12 structural hypothesis: profitable SOL parent habitats may live in A1 topology cells whose dominant breakout visit is H1 or H3 rather than H2.

This does not retune A21. It opens a new preregistered anatomy class using the old A1 atlas only.

## Candidate derivation from old A1 atlas
Eligible atlas cells must:
1. dominant_visit in {H1, H3},
2. topology_supported=true,
3. same_dom_blocks >=4/6,
4. dominant_opportunity_n >=60,
5. clock circular distance >2h from every supported clock {03,15,18},
6. exact hours already economically tested {03,08,11,12,13,15,18} are excluded.

Rank with the frozen A17 anatomy ordering. Select at most two H1-dominant cells and at most two H3-dominant cells, while requiring all selected candidate clocks to be >2h apart. Frozen clock/reference supports come from the A1 topology fields and must preserve the same dominant visit.

No A22 PnL is used in candidate selection.

- Development N>=300,
- PF>1.15 and 5bps PF>1.00,
- raw and stress expectancy/net >0,
- >=4 adequate Development blocks,

Freeze one Development winner by 5bps net, 5bps PF, raw net, N, anatomy rank.

OOS requires exact candidate PF>1 and net>0 raw+stress in External and Reference Validation, plus >=3/4 positive topology-support cells raw and stress.

No OOS retuning. A supported parent habitat does not inherit H2/H3 recovery.
